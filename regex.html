    Regular expressions and finite automata

    A language is regular exactly when a regular expression describes it, and exactly when a finite automaton recognizes it. This page lets you move between the two the way Sipser does in Chapter 1.3: build an NFA from any regular expression, piece by piece, and turn any NFA into a regular expression by removing states from a generalized NFA.

    Writing expressions

    TypeMeans
    a|b or a∪ba or b (union)
    aba followed by b

    ∅ or \emptysetthe empty language

    Regex → NFA

    1. Type an expression, for example (a|b)*abb.
    2. Press Step by step. Each small piece gets its own NFA, and the pieces join with ε-moves for union, concatenation and star.
    3. Use the Convert tab to turn the NFA into a DFA.

    NFA → regex

    1. Draw an NFA or DFA, or open one from the finite automata page.
    2. Press Step by step under “Drawing → regex”. A new start and accept state are added, then states are removed one at a time.
    3. Each removal shows the rule R₁R₂*R₃ ∪ R₄ with the real labels.
