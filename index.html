    Draw DFA and NFA diagrams online

    DeltaDraw is a free finite automata drawing tool. Sketch a deterministic (DFA) or nondeterministic (NFA) finite automaton, test strings on it, convert an NFA to a DFA, minimize it, and export a clean state diagram as PNG, SVG or LaTeX (TikZ). It runs in your browser with nothing to install and no sign-up.

    How to draw

    1. Add a state: double-click empty space, or pick the State tool and click.
    2. Add a transition: hold Shift and drag from one state to another, or use the Transition tool. Drag back onto the same state for a self-loop.
    3. Start arrow: tick Initial state in the panel, or Shift-drag from empty space onto a state.
    4. Accepting state: double-click a state, or tick Accepting state.
    5. Label: select something and type. Separate symbols with commas, like 0,1.
    6. Curve an arrow: drag the middle of a transition. Drag a self-loop to rotate it.
    7. Export: press Export and download PNG, SVG, LaTeX or JFLAP.

    Features

    • DFA, NFA and ε-NFA state diagrams with curved arrows and self-loops
    • Step-by-step string simulation with highlighted states
    • NFA to DFA conversion (subset construction)
    • DFA minimization
    • Automatic transition table
    • Export to PNG, SVG, LaTeX/TikZ and JFLAP (.jff)
    • Open JFLAP files, save and reopen your own drawings
    • Auto-save in your browser, light and dark theme
    Is DeltaDraw free?
    Yes. It is free and open source, and your drawings stay in your own browser.
    Can I use the diagrams in a LaTeX report?
    Yes. Export as LaTeX to get TikZ code, or export SVG or a high-resolution PNG.
    Does it work with JFLAP?
    You can open JFLAP .jff finite automaton files and export your drawing back to .jff.
